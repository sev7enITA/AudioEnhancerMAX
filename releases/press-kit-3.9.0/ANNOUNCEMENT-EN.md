# AudioEnhancerMAX 3.9.0 adds a local Music brief assistant to its free Mac audio workflow

AudioEnhancerMAX 3.9.0 brings its Music brief assistant into the stable release for Apple Silicon Macs. The free, MIT-licensed application combines local audio cleanup, transcription and reviewable speech editing with a text workspace for planning music prompts.

The new assistant offers six original presets, editable styles and arrangement directions, a separate lyrics field, and JSON export that preserves the source inputs alongside the edited prompt. The core builder needs no model or network connection. Optional Ollama suggestions use an installed local model, require permission and remain separate until the user reviews and appends them.

Music brief prepares text; it does not generate music or call a MiniMax service. Drafts remain in the current session, so users should copy or export before closing.

The stable release retains the existing stable data folder. Experimental 1 remains available as a separate preview installation, and the previous stable 3.8.3 is retained for rollback. The direct package targets Apple Silicon and macOS 13 or later. Transcription models may download on first use, then run locally. Some voice and neural engines require separate components; optional Microsoft Edge TTS sends the submitted text to Microsoft's service.

AudioEnhancerMAX is developed by Fabrizio Degni. Its review tools preserve originals, expose speech-edit decisions and attach processing evidence to exported audio. Loudness guidance and file hashes support review; they do not certify listening quality or transcription accuracy.

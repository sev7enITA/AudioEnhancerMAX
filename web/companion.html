A quick voice edit can stay entirely on your phone. A Mac session gives you another processing route. Worker mode lets the phone contribute to a Mac job while the app stays open.

A recording in.
Your edit out.

Trim an interview excerpt, adjust the gain and try Voice EQ. Compare the result with the source before sharing a separate WAV.

No Mac connection, account or cloud service is needed for local editing. Choose a format filter before opening the picker. Local editing supports mono or stereo recordings up to 64 MiB decoded. Read codec, size and export limits.

  1. Import and selectOpen your recording from Files. Review its waveform and choose the start and end of the section you want.
  2. Improve and compareAdjust gain, apply Voice EQ and optionally normalize the peak to −1 dBFS. Switch between A · Original and B · Result at the same playback position.
  3. Export a copyShare a separate WAV, or explicitly prepare an untrimmed supported MP4 copy with replacement AAC audio. The external source file is preserved, and your latest imported workspace stays on the device.

Make an edit here.
Continue over there.

Send either the imported original or your current mobile result to a paired Mac. Choose Voice EQ + level or Level only, then bring the result back.

Requires the updated Mac backend and both devices on the same trusted local network. Use the direct Mac 4.1 BETA for the companion gateway. Access is limited to the session’s imports and jobs.

  1. Pair with your MacStart a companion session in Mac Settings, then paste its private pairing code into the phone. The connection verifies the Mac’s certificate.
  2. Choose the audio and sendContinue from your current edit or return to the untouched original. Select the Mac processing profile and start the job explicitly.
  3. Recover, compare and exportFollow processing status or recover a pending job after an interruption. Download the audio and matching report, bound by a checked SHA-256.

Your phone joins
the processing team.

Keep the companion open and offer its CPU to a Mac job. Worker mode uses the same portable Voice EQ on iOS, Android and the Mac fallback.

Foreground only. Leaving the app, excessive heat or Stop ends the worker session. Supported chunks are up to 2 MiB; interrupted or oversized chunks return to the Mac. This is not continuous background processing.

  1. Pair and opt inConnect the phone. Enable trusted-LAN processing on the Mac and select Auto EQ for the Mac job.
  2. Start the workerThe phone requests work over the encrypted connection. Its status changes from waiting to processing, with completed chunks counted.
  3. Stay in controlStop on the phone or revoke the session on the Mac. If the worker becomes unavailable, the Mac handles the remaining chunk with the same EQ.

Find your Mac or phone on Wi-Fi

Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi and open Connections & Edge Cluster on the Mac. The phone is visible for up to 90 seconds when you enable discovery, and becomes invisible when you leave the screen or switch apps.

Use the direct Mac 4.0 BETA. If a guest network isolates devices, switch to a Wi-Fi network where devices can reach each other.

  1. Find a Mac from the phoneStart a pairing session on the Mac, then open Connection → Find nearby Macs on the phone and pick your Mac.
  2. Find a phone from the MacIn the phone’s Connection tab, choose Make this device discoverable on iOS or Make this phone discoverable on Android. On the Mac choose Find nearby phones, select your phone and click Invite. Accept the invitation on the phone.
  3. Compare the codeWhen both devices show the same eight digits, choose Codes match on both. The Mac can also display a pairing QR code for the mobile app to scan.
  4. Disconnect when doneForget the Mac on the phone or choose Stop and revoke on the Mac. Pair again after restarting the Mac session.

Formats and limits

Select the import format before opening the file picker. The companion remembers your choice and checks the file contents and decoder before replacing the current recording. What the picker displays also depends on the file provider.

Both apps support WAV, MP3, M4A/AAC and FLAC. Android offers Ogg/Opus and AMR, while iOS offers CAF and AIFF. Video import accepts supported MP4 and MOV containers with 3GP also offered on Android. Decoding depends on the device and codec. The local editing limit is 100 MiB source and 64 MiB decoded mono or stereo audio at 8–96 kHz. The separate Mac transfer uses a 20 MiB WAV limit.

For supported H.264/AAC video, an edit that keeps the original frame count, sample rate and channel count can export a separate MP4 with replacement AAC audio. Trimmed audio exports as WAV. Extra metadata, subtitle and data tracks are excluded from the new copy. Video stays on the phone when you send audio to the Mac.

On-device media inspection

Verify Content on your phone

Verify Content shows the file fingerprint, available media metadata and C2PA Content Credentials on your device. When a supported media file contains eligible JPEG cover artwork, Verify can read camera and exposure fields.

See the codec, duration and supported track details before you decide how to use a file. The Studio import format guide lists editing requirements for each format.

Before you begin

A few useful answers.

Do I need a Mac for local editing?

No. Studio and the mobile Music brief builder run on the phone. You need a compatible updated Mac build only for Mac jobs and worker mode.

Will the worker keep running in the background?

No. This preview contributes processing while the app is open. It stops when the app leaves the foreground or the device becomes too warm. The Mac handles an interrupted chunk.

Which Mac version supports the 4.0 companion?

Install the direct Mac 4.0 BETA and use the local pairing guide.

Is this the full desktop editor on a phone?

The preview focuses on one local audio workspace, straightforward improvements and an explicit Mac connection. It is not a multitrack editor or a remote browser for your entire Mac library. Worker mode currently supports Voice EQ only.

What has been tested?

Wi-Fi pairing and audio transfer were checked on a Mac and test environments for iOS and Android. Testing on physical phones, additional file providers and network environments continues. See verification status.

Where can I get the iOS app?

The iOS source for build 10 is public. TestFlight and App Store distribution are pending.

A small, repeatable test

  1. Choose 20-60 seconds of your own non-sensitive speech, or a synthetic recording you are allowed to use. Keep a copy of the original.
  2. Run Smart Enhance. Compare the original and processed audio at a similar listening level. Note any improvement or unwanted change.
  3. In the Precision Speech Editor, inspect one proposed edit. Keep or change it, preview it, then undo and redo. Note whether the decision was clear.
  4. Export a WAV and its processing report or Project Capsule. Confirm the exported audio contains the edits you intended and that you can understand the recorded settings.
  5. For direct 3.9.0 only: build a Music brief, edit its direction and export JSON before closing. This creates text, not music.

It is useful to report a blocked step or no audible improvement. You do not need to give a positive review, upload a recording or test optional network services.

Prepare your feedback

The form stays in this browser tab. It does not submit your entries to this website. Generate a report, review it, then choose whether to save it or open a public GitHub issue. Do not include private recordings, names, transcripts, file paths or secrets.

Review before sharing

GitHub will receive the report text when you open its issue editor. It is published only when you submit it there. You can instead keep the downloaded report private.

How feedback will be used

Reports can inform bug fixes, clearer controls and documentation. Listening notes are subjective observations, not proof of universal quality. Public issue content is visible to other people; there is no automatic publication of this form, and no testimonial is implied.
